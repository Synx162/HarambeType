-> Typing disabled before/after timer:

- typing_active goes high only on timer_start_pulse (KEY2 edge).
	It goes low on timer_expired and on new preload.

-Keyboard acceptance and char_valid are both ANDed with typing_active.


-> Per-char red/green:

- typed_char_valid drives comparator and stats.

- prev_char_errors / char_errors delta ⇒ current_char_correct.

- draw_color_kb_colored chooses COLOR_CORRECT vs COLOR_INCORRECT for all glyph pixels for that char.


-> Stats phases:

- game_active -> in-game phase (to draw live stats continuously).

- post_game_phase -> latched after timer expires (use for one-time end-screen stats + different background).
